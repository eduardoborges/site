Yeah, I made a blog. I honestly have no idea what I'll write here. At first I wanted to write in English to practice (it's not great) and because it's the standard these days. But that's exactly the problem, there's already tons of content in English out there.

I think writing in Portuguese gives me more freedom of expression. I want to cover complex topics in a light, understandable way and, of course, with some dumb jokes. This is the English translation, so bear with me.

Don't expect it to always be serious stuff either. I might post something like a cool Mac app I found, who knows. I'll treat this more like a hobby, I always liked writing and I'm picking it back up as a kind of therapy.

Cheers, Edu.
